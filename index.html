The coach fits in your pocket. The excuses don't.

Arm it once at the venue and put the phone away. ConeCanvas {{ pt.tx }}

Draw the course. It counts the cones.

Before this, course design meant CorelDRAW or honest-to-goodness AutoCAD — somewhere north of $500 of professional CAD, we've been afraid to check — aimed at a parking lot. None of it knows what a slalom is. ConeCanvas does: trace your lot to scale from satellite, snap on parametric slaloms, sweepers, and gates, let the 42-check Course Doctor nag you about escape routes, and print both maps while it keeps the cone count honest.

sunday-course-final-FINAL-v2.acx — 214 cones

For the designer, and the crew stuck setting it up

To-scale satellite tracing
Calibrate the lot from a satellite view instead of eyeballing parking spaces. Save the venue once, design there forever.
Elements that know themselves
Slaloms, sweepers, gates, Chicago boxes — parametric, draggable, and aware of the rulebook. Not twelve anonymous circles.
Live cone count
The setup crew knows exactly how many cones to load before you've finished drawing. They will still forget the timing lights.
Course Doctor
42 checks against SCCA rules and design guidance — speeds, spacing, escape routes — so tech inspection is pleasantly boring.
Print-ready PDF, twice
One map for the drivers' meeting, one for the crew with counts and measurements. No CAD, no subscriptions, no eyeballing.
Plays with the family
Exports to SpeedStream for timing and to Assetto Corsa for pre-driving. The three racing products are, in fact, one scheme.

AC Autocross Maker {{ pt.tx }}

Walk the course Sunday. Drive it Wednesday.

A pipeline that reads the club's official course-map PDF and rebuilds the event inside Assetto Corsa — real venue, real layout, 523 knockable cones, every one within 0.6 meters of where the chalk says. By the time everyone else gets their course walk, you've had thirty runs. Results so far: perfect knowledge of the course, same mid-pack finish. The bottleneck has been identified. It drives.

NASA Crows Landing · via Content Manager · socks optional but traditional

PDF in. Track out.

Reads the official map
Vector cone data extracted straight from the event PDF, fitted to the venue with 0.60 m median error. No tracing, no guessing.
No Blender required
A pure-Python kn5 writer places every cone directly into the game's format. One editable course.json is the whole source of truth.
Knockable cones
All 523 of them, physically movable, emotionally significant. Point-to-point timing gates, just like the real Sunday.
Hard-won physics
Pointer cones rest at exactly 13.54° — we measured 68 real ones to get it right. Yes, there was a version where they all fell over.

Glovebox {{ pt.tx }}

Every fill-up. Every dollar. Every regret.

A logbook for every car the family drives — owned, loaned, or rented. Logging a fill-up takes ten seconds at the pump, works offline, and syncs to the whole household; charge sessions, repairs, and insurance go in the same ledger. Then the insights engine does the math you were avoiding. It was commissioned to defend the sports car. It is now the prosecution's best witness.

$0.00 · introductory price household plan: also $0.00
ten seconds, gloves optional

Accounting, but make it honest

MPG you can't lie to
Full-fill-to-full-fill windows only. A partial fill extends the window instead of inventing a heroic 43 MPG tank.
Gas and electrons
Fuel logs and charge sessions side by side — $/mile for the Spyder vs the EV, settled with data instead of vibes.
Tag your shame
Tag entries track, road trip, canyon — and stats segment by them. The racing tank stops being an outlier once it's labeled.
The ownership ledger
Money in, minus money out, divided by miles owned. The only equation that matters at exit, kept current while you hold.
The whole fleet
Owned, loaners, rentals — rentals spin up for a trip and roll into family totals without polluting any car's stats.
Own your data
CSV in, CSV out, losslessly. It replaces your spreadsheet without holding it hostage. Paste a year of Apple Notes; it copes.
