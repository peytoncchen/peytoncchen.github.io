iOS · family fleet

Glovebox

A logbook for every car the family drives. Ten-second fill-up logging at the pump, MPG math you can't lie to, and a cost ledger that found out the sports car costs $19.84 a mile and simply will not let it go.

Replaces one spreadsheet and several Apple Notes of shame. Knows the Costco habit saves $0.95/gal. Respects the hustle.

Altrove

Like a flight tracker, but for the whole trip. Log where the family has been, follow each other's trips live, and plan where you're all going next — in one shared place instead of four group chats.

Named after the Italian for "elsewhere," which is where we'd all rather be.
